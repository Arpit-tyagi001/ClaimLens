PART 2 - DEFINITIONS
2.1. Pre-Existing Disease means any condition, ailment or injury for which the insured person was diagnosed or received medical advice or treatment within 36 months before the first policy was issued.
2.2. Day Care Treatment means medical treatment or a surgical procedure that is undertaken under general or local anaesthesia in a hospital in less than 24 hours.

PART 3 - WAITING PERIODS
3.1. Initial Waiting Period: The Company shall not be liable for any claim arising within the first 90 days from the date of first inception of this policy, other than claims arising out of an accident.
3.2. Specific Disease Waiting Period: Treatment of cataract, benign prostatic hypertrophy, hernia, hysterectomy and joint replacement is covered only after 12 months of continuous coverage from the date of first inception.
3.3. Pre-Existing Disease Waiting Period: Expenses for any pre-existing disease are covered only after 48 months of continuous coverage from the date of first inception.

PART 4 - GENERAL EXCLUSIONS
4.1. Treatment for any dental condition, unless it requires hospitalisation following an accident.
4.2. Expenses for vitamins, tonics and nutritional supplements, unless prescribed as part of hospitalisation treatment.
4.3. Any treatment taken outside India.

PART 5 - SUB-LIMITS
5.1. Room rent and boarding charges are payable up to Rs. 4,000/- per day.
5.2. Cataract surgery is payable up to Rs. 25,000/- per eye per policy year.

PART 6 - CLAIMS
6.1. The insured person must notify the Company of a planned hospitalisation at least 72 hours before admission.
6.2. All claim documents must reach the Company within 15 days of discharge from hospital.
